# Mike F. Robbins

Mike F. Robbins is an automation and technical writing professional with decades of experience in
information technology. He spent six years as a Microsoft MVP before joining Microsoft, where he
spent another six years as the lead technical writer for Azure PowerShell and Azure CLI, helping
customers turn complex cloud technologies into practical, approachable solutions.

With deep expertise in PowerShell, scripting, automation, Azure, and technical documentation, Mike
has built a career around simplifying complex technology, improving efficiency, and sharing
practical knowledge with others.

Mike is a published author and contributor to several books, including:

- Author of [PowerShell 101: The No-Nonsense Guide to Windows PowerShell][powershell-101]
- Creator of [The PowerShell Conference Book][the-powershell-conference-book]
- Co-author of [Windows PowerShell TFM 4th Edition][windows-powershell-tfm-4]
- Contributing author in the [PowerShell Deep Dives][powershell-deep-dives] book

He continues to share his experience, technical insights, and practical guidance through his blog at
[mikefrobbins.com][mikefrobbins-com] and with the broader technology community on
[LinkedIn][mikefrobbins-linkedin].
